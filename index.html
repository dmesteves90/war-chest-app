Reading links from shops
Paste a product link on its own line and Quick edit reads the name, price and brand. Shops do not let other web pages read them directly, so this needs a small proxy of your own (scripts/page-proxy-worker.js, free on Cloudflare). Only the link goes to it. Saved on this device only.
Add from a shop page (bookmarklet)
Works on most shops. Drag this button to your bookmarks bar: Add to War Chest
Then open a product page on any shop and click the bookmark. War Chest opens in a new tab with the name, price, brand, description and pictures from that page, with tick boxes. Nothing is saved until you press Apply. Pictures are kept exactly as the shop serves them (no re-compressing). The page is read where you are and handed straight to this app: nothing goes through any server. Some shops block bookmarklets or copying pictures; the details still come through without the pictures.
